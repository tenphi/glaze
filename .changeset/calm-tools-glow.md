Ship a harness for coding agents in the package. With [harnesspm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@tenphi/harnesspm), projects that depend on Glaze get a short rule, the docs index, a `glaze-theme` skill and two tools: `glaze:inspect` resolves a theme or palette in light, dark and high contrast and checks every contrast floor, and `glaze:contrast` measures two colors with WCAG and APCA.
