Dark variants of `mode: 'auto'` tone-delta colors (`tone: '+N'` / `'-N'`) now step evenly. A dark delta used to start from the base's light tone, which carries the light window's lift — or, on a contrast-solved base or a mix, sits wherever light put it — so on a tone-50 base `'+4'` came out −5.6 in dark and `'-4'` +1.3, and a `'-4'` hover on an AAA button stepped darker in dark as well. It now starts from where the base sits on the authored scale: its authored tone, or, for a contrast-solved base, a mix, or a `'max'` / `'min'` on a base, its dark tone read back through the dark window. Every dark step is the dark window's scale times the delta (about 0.87× at the defaults: `'+4'` → −3.5, `'-4'` → +3.5) on every base that inverts in dark — along chains, for `from` colors, and for `glaze.color()` links (including a base with its own `lightTone`) — and high-contrast steps are exact, custom `eps` included. On a `fixed` or `static` base the inversion still follows the dependent's own mode. The flip is still decided on the light tone, so a negative delta on a base authored below |N| stops at the dark window's top (`'-4'` on tone 2 steps about +1.7 in dark, on tone 0 not at all). Dark variants of these colors shift — up to about 4 tones on low bases, more on a contrast-solved base — along with anything solved, mixed, or lit from them. Light variants are unchanged; high-contrast variants change only for deltas on contrast-solved, mixed, or extreme bases, or under a custom light `eps`; `contrastLevel: 100` still equals the high-contrast tier.
